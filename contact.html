Contact

Get in touch

How to reach us

Email is the way in. The men’s club is at rathminesmensbasketballclub@gmail.com, the ladies’ club at rathminesbasketballclub@gmail.com. Two separate inboxes, so pick the one that matches. Sponsorship goes to the men’s address.

Instagram is quicker: @rathmines_bc for the men, @rathminesballerz for the ladies. The ladies are also on Facebook. There is no contact form, no phone number and no postal address. An email or a DM is it.

The venue

Where we play

Home games are at St Mary’s on Lower Rathmines Road. The league runs from October to the end of March and most home fixtures are on a Friday night. The photograph below is the hall, and the map beside it is the exact spot.

What to expect

What happens if you get in touch

Both inboxes are read by club members, not an office. Everyone has a day job, so a reply takes a few days rather than the same afternoon. Nobody is ignoring you.

Summer is the quiet stretch. Every other month is much the same: if it is about playing, you will be told when the next trial is, and if it is about sponsorship, one of us will come back to you directly.
