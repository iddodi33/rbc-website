Purple Rain (Flash)

Our continuity offence. Cut, recut and fill.

Set

1 at the top, 2 and 3 on the wings, 4 and 5 on the low blocks.

Sequence

  1. 4 comes off 5’s baseline screen to the right corner.
  2. 1 passes to 2 on the left wing, then cuts through off 5’s screen to the left corner.
  3. 5 flashes to the high post. 2 passes to 5. 4 lifts from the corner to the right wing, 3 fills the top.
  4. 1 lifts to the left wing. 2 cuts backdoor, round under the basket, to the right corner.
  5. 5 passes out to 3 at the top.
  6. 5 drops to screen for 2, who cuts along the baseline to the left corner.
  7. 3 passes to 4 on the right wing, then cuts through off 5’s screen to the right corner.
  8. 5 flashes to the high post, 4 passes to 5. 2 lifts to the left wing, 1 fills the top.
  9. 3 lifts from the corner to the right wing. 4 cuts along the baseline to the left corner.
  10. 5 passes out to 1 at the top. Reset and run it again.

Rules

  • Pass and cut through, never pass and stand.
  • The 5 is the hub: flash to the high post, screen the baseline cutter, pass out to reset.
  • Whoever vacates a spot gets it filled: corner lifts to the wing, wing fills the top.
  • Shot by around 8 seconds on the shot clock.

West

Against a 2-3 zone. Gets either an open three for your best shooter or a quick basket inside. Can be run to either side.

If the clock is not a factor, 1 passes to 2 and 2 passes back to 1 to make the zone shift. If the clock is a factor, skip those two passes and go straight into it.

  1. 3 drops down toward the baseline.
  2. 1 dribbles a little to the left, 2 cuts underneath and through to the left corner-wing area for a possible pass from 1 and a shot.
  3. At the same time 5 screens the middle low defender (X5) and 3 screens the opposite low defender (X4). 4 cuts under both screens to the ballside block.
  4. Read: if X3 steps out to cover 2, the quick pass from 1 to 4 is open for a layup. If X3 stays inside, 2 has the open three, or can feed the post.
