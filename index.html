Who we are

A club that is easy to join and hard to leave

Rathmines Basketball Club runs senior teams in two divisions. Training is weekly, games are at the weekend, and the standard ranges from people who played underage to people who picked a ball up last year.

There is no trial gauntlet and no membership committee. If you live locally and want a run, come down. The club exists because a group of people wanted somewhere to play that felt like the area it is named after.
